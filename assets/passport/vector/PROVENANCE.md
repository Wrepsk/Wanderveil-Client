# Vector passport plates

Generated on 19 October 2026 by [`tools/passport-art`](../../../tools/passport-art/README.md) from
hand-authored SVG sources in that folder. The palettes, layouts, and motifs (cover borders, crests,
banner pennant, schematic map, guilloche paper, identity/portrait framing) were adapted from the
user's "Eorzea Passport" Claude Design canvas. The stamp impressions and page-stack details are new
for this repository. No game textures, extracted pixels, third-party logos, or upstream artwork are
included; nation names and heraldic ideas are attributable to Square Enix and are interpreted, not
copied. These plates are project assets under the repository license.

The symbol plate (`symbols.png`) reuses the crests and emblems as flat masks; its twelve motifs are new for this repository.

Every `editions/<edition>/` folder plus `book-cover.png`, `demo-portrait.png`, `stamp-atlas.png`, `symbols.png`, and `grain.png` (generated noise for the pencil) are embedded
in the plugin, one edition per choosable passport. The map is
schematic, not validated geography, and the portrait is an anonymous placeholder.

The earlier image-generated plates in `assets/passport/` and `assets/passport/studies/` are kept
for comparison and gallery checks, but are no longer embedded.
